Don't-Lie vs Langfuse / Helicone / Arize / Opik

We don't replace LLM observability. We do one thing the others don't: signed, hash-linked receipts that survive provider changes and audit review.

Why this page exists

When to choose Don't-Lie

You're a regulated-industry AI engineer who needs to hand a customer, auditor, or regulator an offline-verifiable record of what the AI said. You don't want to send them a login link to a hosted dashboard they can't audit.

Specifically:

  • Healthcare scribe team: clinician asked "what did the AI tell the patient?"
  • Legal-tech team: court asked for evidence of an AI-assisted brief.
  • Financial-services team: regulator asked for proof of decision provenance.
  • Internal security team: anyone with DB write access could rewrite the AI transcript; you want a chain that detects it.

When to choose something else

  • You need full tracing with span trees per request → use Langfuse or Arize.
  • You need eval scoring at scale → use Arize or Opik.
  • You need a managed hosted dashboard and have no audit pressure → any of them works.

What we don't claim

We don't claim the model is correct, that the provider is the one in the receipt, or that the signing key is operated by a particular person. We prove one thing: the bytes you signed are still the bytes in the bundle. That's a narrow claim and a strong one.
