Honest scope

What a receipt proves. And what it doesn't.

We're not a fact-checker. We don't claim the model was correct. We claim something narrower and provable.

PROVES

The receipt was signed by the documented key.

Every receipt embeds the Ed25519 public key that signed it. Anyone can re-verify offline.

PROVES

The chain is unbroken from the first receipt.

Each receipt's parent_sha256 points to the one before. Reorder, drop, or insert — verification fails.

DOES NOT PROVE

The model was correct or truthful.

Hallucinations are signed faithfully. That's the whole point — use receipts as evidence of what, not of whether.

DOES NOT PROVE

Which person held the signing key.

We record the key. Identity comes from your org — HSM, SSO, audit trail of who controls which key.

Your data. Your custody.

We never see your prompts. Neither does anyone else.

The most common question we get from HIPAA and FinTech buyers is some version of: "Are my prompts sitting in plain text on your servers?" The honest answer: there are no servers. The vault lives on the laptop it's signed on. You can put it on an encrypted volume, hash-only mode, or your own S3 — and the signature still works.

Local-first by default

Every receipt is signed on the machine that made the call. The vault is a single vault.jsonl file on your disk. We have no service that receives your prompts, no logs we own, no "trust us."

Hash-only mode

The vault can store only the SHA-256 of each prompt/response — never the plaintext. The signature still anchors the chain to the original bytes. Switch with one flag:

$ dontlie config --payload-mode=hash

Drop-in on encrypted volumes

vault.jsonl is just a file. Put it on FileVault (macOS), LUKS (Linux), BitLocker (Windows), or an encrypted EBS volume in AWS. The receipt format doesn't care where it lives.

Bring-your-own object lock

Pro and above can write the bundle directly to S3 with Object Lock in compliance mode. Even AWS can't tamper with it after the lock window closes. Your auditor gets a URL; you keep the key.

How it works

Three steps.
One local-first vault.

  1. 01

    Intercept

    Point your client at http://localhost:8768 instead of the upstream provider. Don't-Lie proxies every request, captures the canonical payload, and forwards it unchanged.

    • OpenAI, Anthropic, local models, anything HTTP/JSON
    • Streaming and non-streaming responses both supported
    • Zero code change in the calling app
    POST/v1/chat/completions
    modelMiniMax-M3
    messages[3 items]
    → proxycapturing
  2. 02

    Sign

    Each captured receipt is canonicalized (sorted-key JSON), hashed (SHA-256), and signed with an Ed25519 key that never leaves your machine. The signature is appended to the receipt.

    • Ed25519 via your platform's crypto provider
    • Optional key rotation with monotonic key_id
    • Optional co-signature from the hosted witness service
    payloadsha256:a054984…
    key_id7fd3731d…
    signature9JRA95Wh…
  3. 03

    Link & verify

    Every receipt records parent_sha256 — the hash of the previous receipt. To verify, the auditor walks the chain forward, checking each signature and parent link. One tampered byte anywhere breaks the rest.

    • Single self-contained JSONL bundle
    • One command: dontlie verify --verbose
    • No network, no API keys, no clock skew to argue about
    depth1,047 receipts
    verify time0.51s
    resultVERIFIED
  4. 04

    Share

    One URL proves the receipt — no Dont-Lie account, no Dont-Lie server, no installer on the verifier's side. Paste the URL into a chat, an email, or a court exhibit. The recipient's browser verifies the Ed25519 signature entirely client-side.

    • Self-contained: the receipt + public key live in the URL hash
    • Zero network calls during verification (PLDG.md)
    • Works on a clean laptop, an air-gapped forensic workstation, or a phone

Witness co-signature

Optional hosted notary co-signs each receipt hash with an independent Ed25519 key. Adds a second party who can attest "yes, this hash reached us at this time."

HSM-backed customer keys + rotation

Compliance tier pins the operator key to an HSM (AWS CloudHSM, GCP KMS, Azure Key Vault). Keys rotate on a monotonic key_id — old receipts verify against the old public key, new ones against the new. The chain keeps the rotation history.

  • key_id embedded in every receipt
  • operator_pub_sha256 pins the public key
  • rotation_tx in the bundle header

Multi-region notaries

us-east · eu-west · apac. Pin the witness to a region inside your data-residency boundary. Each receipt gets a notarization from the region you choose.
