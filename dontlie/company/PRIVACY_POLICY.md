# Don't-Lie — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-07-24

## What this policy covers
This policy covers Don't-Lie — the local-first, signed-receipt vault for AI
calls. It applies to the open-source Software and to any hosted services we
operate (Pro, Team, Enterprise tiers).

## The Software (free, local install)
The free Software runs entirely on your machine. We do not operate a server
that receives your data. The Software writes to a local SQLite file you
control. **We see nothing.** If you don't believe us, the source is open
and the receipt bundle is verifiable offline.

## Hosted services (Pro, Team, Enterprise)
If you use a paid tier, the following applies:

**Data we collect:**
- Account email and authentication tokens.
- Billing information (handled by our payment processor; we never see
  your card number).
- Cloud-synced receipt data, only if you opt in. Sync is end-to-end
  encrypted with a key derived from your password; we cannot read it.

**Data we do NOT collect:**
- The contents of your prompts or responses, unless you opt in to sync.
- Your signing keys. Keys never leave your machine.

**Where data is stored:**
- Cloud sync is hosted in [region TBD, default: US]. Enterprise customers
  may select EU or self-host.

**Retention:**
- Account data: while your account is active + 30 days after deletion.
- Receipt data: until you delete it or your account is closed.

**Your rights:**
- Export everything you have in Don't-Lie at any time (`dontlie export`).
- Delete your account and all associated data: see [contact channel].
- Object to processing, request portability, request correction: same.

## Cookies and tracking
The Software has no analytics SDK. Hosted services may use a privacy-
respecting analytics tool (Plausible or self-hosted Matomo) to count page
views. No cross-site tracking, no fingerprinting, no third-party trackers.

## Children
Don't-Lie is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect
data from children.

## Changes to this policy
We will post material changes at least 30 days in advance. Continued use
after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
