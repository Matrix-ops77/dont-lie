# Don't-Lie — Brand

**Last updated:** 2026-07-24

## Name
**Don't-Lie**. The hyphen is part of the name. Pronounce: "don't-LIE"
(verb, imperative). The name says what the product does: it makes lying
detectable after the fact. It does not claim to prevent the model from
lying in the first place. That distinction is the brand.

## Color
- **Primary:** `#0F172A` (ink — slate-900). Wordmark, headlines, buttons.
- **Accent:** `#22D3EE` (cyan-400). Glyph, "live" indicator, "Proven" check.
- **Warn:** `#F59E0B` (amber-500). Only for "Not-proven" callouts in report.
- **Paper:** `#F8FAFC` (slate-50). Background.
- **Ink-soft:** `#475569` (slate-600). Body text.

Dark mode: paper `#020617`, ink `#E2E8F0`, accent stays `#22D3EE`.

## Type
- **Display / wordmark:** Inter Tight, 700, tight tracking.
- **Body / UI:** Inter, 400 / 500.
- **Code / hashes / signatures:** JetBrains Mono, 400, 0.85em.
- **Sizes:** H1 2.0rem, H2 1.4rem, body 1rem, code 0.85rem.

## Voice
**Terse, honest, technical, not clever.**

- One-sentence answers to "what does it do." "It signs AI calls."
- Never claim truth. Say "signed," "captured," "verifiable." Not "true" or
  "trusted AI" or "verified answers."
- Active voice. "The proxy signs the call" not "calls are signed."
- Plain words. "tamper-evident" is good. "cryptographically attested
  provenance artifact" is bad.
- Small words for small things. "hash," "key," "chain," "verify."
- Number claims must come from a runnable benchmark (`BENCHMARK.md`).

## Signature glyph
A small `✓` in `#22D3EE`, used: prefix on wordmark, "verified" indicator
on a receipt row, CLI cursor (replaces `>`). Keep it isolated from copy.

## Logo
Wordmark "Don't-Lie" in Inter Tight 700, with `✓` to the left. Min 96px.
Clear space: the height of the `✓` on all sides.

## Don't
- No accent on body text. No drop shadows. No gradients.
- Don't claim "secure" or "trusted" without a verification.
- Don't say "AI safety" — we are evidence, not safety.
- Don't use "solution." Don't use stock photography.

## Voice examples
- ✅ "Signs AI calls. Verifiable offline. Honest about scope."
- ❌ "The most trusted AI governance platform for the modern enterprise."
- ✅ "Receipts: 3 ok, 0 bad."
- ❌ "Your AI is now safe and compliant."
