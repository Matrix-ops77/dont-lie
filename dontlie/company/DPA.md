# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)
**Last updated:** 2026-07-24

This DPA supplements the Don't-Lie Terms of Service when Don't-Lie is a
Processor of Personal Data on behalf of a Customer (Controller). Complies
with GDPR Art. 28, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and equivalent regimes.

## 1. Definitions and scope
GDPR Art. 4 terms apply. "Customer" = entity that accepted the Terms.
"Don't-Lie" = hosted-service provider. For hosted services, Don't-Lie is
the Processor; the Customer is the Controller. The free local Software is
not a GDPR processing relationship; the Customer is the sole Controller
of any data on their own machine.

## 2. Subject matter and duration
Don't-Lie Processes Personal Data only to provide the hosted Service, for
the duration of the subscription.

## 3. Nature and purpose
Capture, store, and serve signed AI-call receipts the Customer syncs;
authenticate users; operate, secure, and bill the Service.

## 4. Categories of data
Account (email, name, auth tokens); receipt data (only fields the Customer
chooses to sync — default excludes prompt/response bodies, includes only
metadata); operational logs (IP, user agent, request ID, timestamp;
retained 30 days).

## 5. Data subjects and controller obligations
Data subjects are determined by the Customer (typically end users whose
interactions the Customer routes through its AI agents). The Customer is
responsible for the lawfulness of underlying Processing, required
consents, and Data Subject rights.

## 8. Processor obligations
Don't-Lie will: process only on documented instructions; bind authorized
persons to confidentiality; implement security in our Security page
(encryption in transit and at rest, access logging, least-privilege);
engage sub-processors only with notice; assist with Data Subject
requests; notify of breaches without undue delay; submit to audits.

## 9. Sub-processors
List at [URL]. 30 days' notice before adding any. Customer may object on
reasonable grounds.

## 10. International transfers
Standard contractual clauses (EU 2021/914) and the UK IDTA for transfers
from the EEA / UK to third countries.

## 11. Liability and precedence
Subject to the limitation-of-liability in the Terms of Service. This DPA
prevails over the Terms on Personal Data processing.
