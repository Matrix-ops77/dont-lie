# Don't-Lie — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-07-24

## What we collect

- **Local software:** nothing. The local proxy runs entirely on your hardware.
- **Hosted service:** the AI receipts you choose to upload, your account
  email, and billing metadata.

## What we do not collect

- We do not log your upstream API keys.
- We do not read your prompts or responses unless you explicitly grant
  access for support.
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

## How we store data

- AES-256-GCM at rest with Argon2id-derived keys.
- TLS 1.3 in transit.
- Backups encrypted with a separate key, retained for 30 days.

## Your rights

- Access: download all your data via `dontlie export`.
- Deletion: terminate your account; data purged within 30 days.
- Portability: portable verification bundles are a first-class feature.

## Subprocessors

- Stripe (payments)
- AWS or GCP (hosting)
- Slack/Teams (alerts)

We will notify you of any new subprocessor at least 30 days in advance.
