# Don't-Lie — Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This DPA supplements the Terms of Service and applies when Don't-Lie
processes personal data on behalf of the customer ("Controller").

## 1. Roles

- **Controller:** the customer.
- **Processor:** Don't-Lie, Inc.

## 2. Subject matter and duration

Don't-Lie processes the customer's AI receipts for the duration of the
service agreement.

## 3. Nature of processing

Storage, retrieval, signing, verification, and export of AI receipts.

## 4. Types of data

- Account email and billing data.
- AI receipts (may contain personal data if the customer includes it in
  prompts or responses).

## 5. Subprocessors

See Privacy Policy. Don't-Lie will maintain an up-to-date list and notify
the customer of changes.

## 6. Security measures

- AES-256-GCM at rest.
- TLS 1.3 in transit.
- Argon2id-derived vault keys.
- Quarterly third-party penetration tests.
- Annual SOC 2 Type II audit.

## 7. Customer obligations

- Provide instructions to Don't-Lie through the service interface.
- Ensure the lawful basis for processing (e.g., consent, legitimate
  interest).

## 8. Data subject requests

Don't-Lie will assist the customer in responding to data subject
requests within 30 days.

## 9. Breach notification

Don't-Lie will notify the customer within 72 hours of becoming aware of a
personal data breach.

## 10. Return and deletion

On termination, customer data is deleted within 30 days unless retention
is required by law.

## 11. Governing law

Delaware, USA.
