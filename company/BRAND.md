# Don't-Lie — Brand

## Name
Don't-Lie. Two words. Capital D, capital L. With hyphen.

## Voice
- Honest, calm, technical.
- Never claim what we don't prove.
- Never say "truth verified" or "AI is true."
- Preferred language: "signature valid," "chain intact," "record changed,"
  "not established by this receipt."

## Type
- Display: Inter or system-ui sans-serif, 600 weight.
- Body: same family, 400 weight.
- Mono: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular for IDs, hashes, signatures.

## Color
- Verified: dark green `#166534`.
- Failed: dark red `#B91C1C`.
- Neutral / not-proven: slate `#475569`.
- Background: white. Text: near-black `#0F172A`.

## Logo
- Wordmark + small lock-in-journal icon (TBD).
- Single color: `#0F172A` on light, `#FFFFFF` on dark.

## Don't
- No gradients.
- No "AI is truthful" claims.
- No checkmarks on non-proven claims.
- No testimonials about safety without receipts to back them.

## Do
- Show receipts.
- Show chains.
- Show tamper detection.
- Show what we don't prove.
